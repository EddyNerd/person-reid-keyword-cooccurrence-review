# Cumulative and historical figure paths

These files preserve existing public figure addresses. The current article
figures and captions are in [manuscript_figures](../manuscript_figures/).

The cumulative 2015-2022 and 2015-2024 drawings now follow the successive fixed
GEM-coordinate convention. The former inherits 58 positions from 2015-2020,
31 additional positions from the archived 2015-2024 drawing and two new
fixed-anchor GEM placements. Five reinstated terms are aligned in the 2024
drawing so that all 89 shared displayed terms have exactly equal coordinates.
Graphs, frequencies, pruning rules and saved communities are unchanged.

See the [coordinate verification, data and rendering sources](../corrections/cumulative_fixed_GEM_20261116/README.md).
The earlier drawings remain identified in [archive](../archive/README.md).
