Figure 4. Correspondence between archived Louvain L1 communities in 2019⁠–⁠2020 and 2021⁠–⁠2022.
Note. The diagram includes 42 normalized keyword tokens visible in both historical filter-and-suppress graphs. Ribbon widths and bar heights represent shared-token counts, not publication counts. Each colour identifies a source community and continues into the destination bars. The same overlap count is repeated at both ends of each ribbon. Community identifiers are local to each period; matching identifiers do not imply unchanged membership. Communities are positioned to reduce crossings. The 2019⁠–⁠2020 community C3 contains 22 saved tokens, of which 14 remain visible in 2021⁠–⁠2022. These shared tokens are distributed across three destination communities: C0 contains base, data, surveil and video (4); C1 contains id, learn, metric, recognit, supervis and vision (6); and C2 contains adapt, domain, label and unsupervis (4). The other eight tokens are outside this shared-token comparison; their absence from the later drawing does not establish absence from the corpus. The historical random seed was not recorded.
