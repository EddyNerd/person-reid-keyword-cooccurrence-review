Figure 7. Correspondence between archived Louvain L1 communities in 2021⁠–⁠2022 and 2023⁠–⁠2024.
Note. The diagram includes 51 normalized keyword tokens visible in both historical filter-and-suppress graphs. Ribbon widths and bar heights represent shared-token counts, not publication counts. Colors identify the 2021⁠–⁠2022 source communities and continue into the segmented destination bars. Each overlap count is repeated at both ends of its ribbon. Community identifiers are local to each period, and communities are positioned to reduce crossings.
The 18 shared members of 2021⁠–⁠2022 C1 are distributed across four destination communities: C1 contains retriev (1); C2 contains attent, mechan, multi, scale and vision (5); C3 contains contrast, deep, id, learn, loss, metric, recognit and supervis (8); and C4 contains convolut, graph, network and neural (4). Meanwhile, cross, infrar, modal and visibl remain together, passing from C3 to C1. These correspondences describe shared-token memberships rather than unchanged communities. Tokens outside the shared set are omitted, without implying their absence from the corpus. The historical random seed was not recorded.
